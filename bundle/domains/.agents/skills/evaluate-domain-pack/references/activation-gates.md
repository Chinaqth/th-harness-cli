# Activation Readiness Gates

Require:

- a validated profession research ledger;
- source IDs on every current artifact and Pack evaluation;
- synchronized registry and manifest identity, version, status, and owner;
- meaningful applicability, routes, and capabilities;
- existing workflow, Skill, evaluator, template, and dependency references;
- named owner and at least one reviewer;
- explicit Kernel compatibility statement;
- reproducible activation evidence stored as existing repository-relative files under
  `changes/`;
- current passing artifact evaluations;
- repository and structural validation;
- representative positive and negative routing evidence;
- final independent score greater than 90 with all hard gates and no P0/P1.

Passing content gates means `content-complete`. Unresolved organization-specific gates produce
`needs-org-input`. Passing both sets means `activation-ready`. Lifecycle activation remains a
reviewed owner action.
